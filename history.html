A BRIEF TIMELINE

Milestones on
the way up.

A few well-known moments from the history of mountaineering.

1786

Mont Blanc is climbed

Jacques Balmat and Michel-Gabriel Paccard make the first recorded ascent of Mont Blanc, a landmark in European mountaineering.

1865

The Matterhorn ascent

Edward Whymper's team reaches the summit of the Matterhorn, a famous moment in the golden age of Alpine climbing.

1953

Everest summit

Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay reach the summit of Mount Everest on 29 May.

New tools, enduring lessons

Modern forecasting, equipment, and training support climbers, while judgment and preparation remain essential.
