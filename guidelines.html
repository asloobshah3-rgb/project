GENERAL GUIDELINES

Plan well.
Come back safe.

Simple reminders for responsible mountain travel. Training and local advice always come first.

01

Research your route

Understand distance, elevation, terrain, access requirements, and escape options. Use reliable maps and current local information.

02

Check conditions

Review forecasts and local advisories shortly before departure. Be willing to change or cancel your plan.

03

Pack for the activity

Carry appropriate layers, water, food, navigation, first-aid supplies, and emergency equipment for the route and conditions.

04

Stay with your team

Agree on roles, communication, turnaround times, and what to do if someone becomes unwell or separated.

05

Respect the environment

Follow local regulations, pack out waste, protect wildlife, and respect local culture and communities.

06

Know when to turn back

Reaching the summit is optional; returning safely is essential. Do not let a goal override sound judgment.

Important: This information is for general education only. Mountaineering can be dangerous. Get qualified training and follow local professional guidance.
