OUR STORY

Built around a
love of mountains.

Alpine Ascents is a mountaineering information portal bringing together educational resources about climbing, mountain environments, preparation, and expedition culture.

Our goal is to make useful information easy to explore. Whether you are learning the basics or researching an expedition, start with preparation, stay curious, and put safety first.
