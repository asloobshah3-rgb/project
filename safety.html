KNOW THE RISKS

Mountain hazards
deserve respect.

This page is educational and is not a substitute for professional training or local safety advice.

01

Weather changes

Wind, storms, cold, and poor visibility can make a route dangerous. Check forecasts and be ready to turn back.

02

Altitude

High altitude can cause serious illness. Ascend gradually where appropriate, know warning signs, and descend for concerning symptoms.

03

Terrain

Loose rock, steep slopes, snow, ice, and falling objects require route-specific knowledge and equipment.

04

Fatigue & dehydration

Manage pace, food, water, rest, and team communication. Do not let summit goals override safety.

SHELTER & CAMPS

A place to rest.
A plan to stay safe.

Choose shelter carefully

Use established campsites where possible and select a location suited to the weather, terrain, and local rules. Protect yourself from wind, cold, and moisture.

Leave no trace

Pack out waste, minimize disturbance, respect wildlife, and follow local fire and camping regulations.

Have an emergency plan

Know how to contact local rescue services, carry appropriate emergency supplies, and share your itinerary with a trusted person.
