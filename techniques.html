CLIMBING STYLES

Different paths
to the top.

Routes vary in terrain, difficulty, exposure, and the experience they require.

↗

Hiking & trekking

Walking routes on trails and mountain terrain. Fitness, navigation, weather awareness, and suitable footwear matter.

FOUNDATION
⌁

Rock climbing

Climbing rock faces using handholds and footholds. Technical routes require appropriate equipment and trained supervision.

TECHNICAL SKILL
❄

Alpine climbing

Combines mountain travel with rock, snow, or ice conditions. Conditions can change quickly and require specialist skills.

ADVANCED PLANNING
△

Expedition climbing

Longer journeys that may involve camps, acclimatization, logistics, and careful management of supplies.
